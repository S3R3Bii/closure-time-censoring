## Exact calculations and fair comparator

Let T be recorded calendar-day closure delay, or infinity when absent under the endpoint; C is cutoff minus creation day. At the hypothetical cutoff observe event ifT<=C,otherwise censor atC. Events precede censors at tied calendar days; both leave the risk set after that day's factor. At dayd, n_d=sum counts with observed time>=d and e_d=observed event count atd. Product-limit S(d)=product overj<=d of(1−e_j/n_j). Use exact rational factors. If a positive survival tail has no risk support before a required day, mark the corresponding RMST NOT_ESTIMABLE; do not carry an unsupported plateau. A zero survival tail can remain zero.

Calendar-day restricted mean is sum ofS(d) for d=0,...,tau−1. The later captured-record benchmark is mean(min(T,tau)) across all eligible requests, assigningtau to absent or later dates under that endpoint. Compare absolute errors on the same capped estimand. The fair completed-case-only comparator is mean(min(T,tau)) among requests with an event observed by the reconstructed cutoff. Also report the traditional uncapped completed-case mean descriptively, but do not compare its error with a different capped target. If no completed cases exist, retain an undefined comparator.

For each censored request, min(T,tau) is bounded by min(C,tau) andtau; observed events contribute their known capped time. Average these to obtain finite-cohort observation bounds. Require the later captured-record benchmark inside those bounds. They bound this reconstructed recorded-date target, not true problem resolution. Report whether the model-based KM estimate lies inside/outside them; do not force it into a bound or hide a failure.

KM's independent-censoring/closure-distribution assumptions may fail when calendar cohorts change, and cannot be established from this snapshot. The benchmark error comparison evaluates the declared reconstruction in these finite panels; it does not prove unbiasedness or future agency performance. Report every cohort's errors and support/closure fractions, plus equal-panel mean absolute error and win/tie counts over estimable held panels. Do not select a favorable endpoint or hide agency/cohort reversals. No sampling confidence interval or inference beyond this captured panel is claimed; exact empirical bounds and endpoint/cutoff sensitivity are retained.
