# Data reuse and privacy boundary

NYC Open Data's official FAQ permits reuse, with portal terms retaining agency authority, revision/no-history and warranty limits. Attribute NYC 311 and the responding agencies without implying endorsement. A current source snapshot is not a historical dashboard or complete request lifecycle.

Retrieve only unique_key,created_date,closed_date,agency,status. These minimized response originals remain outside Git. Do not request names, addresses, coordinates, telephone/email, Problem/Problem Detail, Additional Details or narratives. Publish only aggregated counts by fixed agency/cohort/creation-day/recorded-delay/status-coherence/quality bins, together with query/source hashes and independent extraction certificates. Request identifiers and exact timestamps are excluded from public artifacts. Status text is reduced to Closed/other/missing categories rather than publishing arbitrary strings.

The captured endpoint is a recorded closure date, not verified problem resolution, first closure, reopening history, staff work time or customer experience. No agency personnel or requester is assessed. Independent reviewed manuscript publication is authorized; separate formal-publication projects including Azure optimization remain private.
